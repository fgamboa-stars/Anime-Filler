# Backtest results

Generated by `npm run backtest` on 2026-11-03, from 108 series of curated labels replayed as if each episode were new.

Each mode hides what would not be known at that moment, including arc names, later seasons, and any statistics learned from hidden labels. Confidence bands use out-of-fold calibration: a story group is never scored by a curve fitted on its own episodes.

## Accuracy against the base rate

"Always canon" is the baseline: predicting the most common label. Macro F1 weighs all four labels equally, so it exposes a model that ignores the rarer ones.

| Scenario | What it simulates | Episodes | Accuracy | Always canon | Macro F1 | Baseline F1 |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| next-episode | A new episode arrives after every earlier one was reviewed | 3524 | 84.2% | 68.4% | 73.9% | 20.3% |
| gap-fill | Random episodes are missing; both neighbours are known | 1408 | 85.9% | 71.2% | 75.1% | 20.8% |
| sparse-review | A person reviewed one episode in eight; the rest are predicted in airing order | 6082 | 79.3% | 70.1% | 68.1% | 20.6% |
| batch-tail | The last 30% of a series arrives at once, with nobody reviewing along the way | 2150 | 69.2% | 65.2% | 46.8% | 19.7% |
| new-season | A new season arrives; only earlier seasons have labels | 2691 | 67.4% | 67.5% | 20.1% | 20.1% |
| cold-start | A series with no labels and no related seasons | 7013 | 69.8% | 69.9% | 20.7% | 20.6% |

## Not measured

- **OVA, Special and Movie.** The datasets have no such labels. AniList format settles those, and the 32 episodes in OVA- or Special-format entries are excluded above because their curated labels use a different scheme (for example CANON).
- **Episodes with descriptions.** AniList has none, so everything here is title and metadata only.
